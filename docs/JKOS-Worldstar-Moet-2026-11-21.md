# Worldstar VIP: Moët in Service & Door

Worldstar VIP tickets include one bottle per ticket/table, labelled `Flasche Moët`.
The shared includedSupply function supplies the guest-list pill, preorder entry,
preparation totals and total bottle count. Cocktails and water are not added.
Regular VIP Crémant and paid extras retain their existing rules.

Regression coverage uses the actual ticket spelling `VIP Table - World Star VIP · 2 Guests · Moët & Chandon`, plus multiple tables and a mixed regular VIP booking.
